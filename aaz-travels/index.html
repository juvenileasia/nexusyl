Got Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Aaz Travel different from other travel agencies?
Aaz Travel focuses on personalized service, transparent pricing, and carefully curated travel experiences. Our team works closely with each traveler to ensure every trip is smooth, enjoyable, and stress-free.
Can Aaz Travel help me plan a fully customized itinerary?
Absolutely. Whether you're planning a family vacation, honeymoon, corporate trip, or solo adventure, we design tailor-made itineraries that match your interests, budget, and schedule.
Does Aaz Travel offer group or corporate travel packages?
Yes, we organize group tours, corporate trips, team-building retreats, and special events. We handle transportation, accommodations, activities, and custom requests for all group sizes.
How can I stay updated on new offers and travel promotions?
Follow our social media pages or subscribe to our mailing list to receive updates on discounts, seasonal packages, and exclusive deals.
Does Aaz Travel provide visa and document assistance?
Yes. We offer comprehensive visa support, including documentation guidance, application submission, and appointment scheduling. Requirements vary by country, and our experts will walk you through each step.
What should I know about cancellations or changes to my booking?
We understand plans can change. Contact us as soon as possible, and we will assist based on airline, hotel, and package policies. Refunds or change fees depend on the provider's terms.
Is travel insurance included in your packages?
Travel insurance is optional but highly recommended. We can help you choose the right insurance plan for health coverage, trip protection, and emergency support.
How do I get a quote for my trip?
You can request a quote through our website, WhatsApp, email, or by visiting our office. Share your destination, dates, and preferences, and our team will prepare a detailed quotation for you.
