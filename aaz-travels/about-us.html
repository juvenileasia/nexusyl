Who We Are

Personal Service, Built on Trust

Aaz Travel is a UK-based, IATA accredited travel agency based in Whitechapel, London. We help travellers of all kinds — from families booking their annual holiday to pilgrims planning their Umrah or Hajj — get to where they're going with confidence.

As a small, dedicated team, we believe travel should feel personal. That's why every booking comes with real people you can call, WhatsApp, or visit in person — not just a booking reference number.
